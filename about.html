Our story

More than a coffee stop.

A warm place to take a break, meet a friend, and enjoy a good cup of coffee.

Est. May 2021 · Tanza, Cavite

Started with passion. Shared with everyone.

Café BLK & BRWN began in Barangay Capipisa, Tanza, Cavite. From its original home, the brand has expanded across Cavite and into Metro Manila, including Festival Mall, Alabang.

Our menu brings together espresso-based coffee, chai and chocolate drinks, iced drinks, Freskaró juices, and food such as pasta and sandwiches.
